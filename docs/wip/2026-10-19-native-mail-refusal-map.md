No direct-message production constructor found above is an intentional **recipient policy drop**. Follow idle drop is a distinct `LocalOutcome::Dropped`, and policy does not authorize dropping a hard direct message merely because `retryable` is false. The old pre-release custody mapper collapsed every nonretryable NotSubmitted to DroppedByPolicy (`automation-storage/src/mail_custody_records.rs:224–228`); that implementation is evidence of the old mapping, not evidence that these causes are policy drops.

Recommendation: retain the two RecipientUnknown cases with their normalization order, add a truthful shared recipient-refusal home for unsupported/precondition/native rejection outcomes, and classify open NotSubmitted reasons at their owning native boundary before choosing a terminal shared reason. Do not encode a native rejection as ProcessingFailed merely because the generic library enum lacks a home. At that decision point the Lead owned the variant/mapping decision, and the working consumer mapping was held until it was settled.

Decision 487 adopts RecipientRefused with a validated consumer code, built in library C38/W-C39. Every definite rejection without the specific unknown-recipient meaning uses its own camelCase code. Nonretryable NotSubmitted is classified at its emitter, never by the boolean. Retryable no-effect results hold where allowed; hard steer/guard uses the emitter's refusal code. Genuine input/processing defects remain ProcessingFailed. Decision 488 keeps the public DeliveryReceipt and LocalObservation unchanged: required companion UnsentCause metadata at the internal route/attempt port carries the classification through the actor. No human reason-string matching is authorized. This decision preceded the qualified pin and the component proof recorded below.

Decision507 resolves a discovered emitter/projection contradiction without widening direct Held. Recorded setup Unavailable and CancellationUnresolved carry Refused(nativeUnavailable), preserving their old public nativeRejected receipt. HostLocationsUnavailable carries ProcessingFailure because it is local host-path resolution. Other actual no-effect availability emitters still use Holdable; the variant remains used. Direct InvalidInput and ProcessingFailure map to ProcessingFailed with invalidPayload and executionFailed respectively. Follow keeps decision484 exactly: accepted is Delivered, uncertain is Uncertain, retryable NotSubmitted is indefinite Held, and every other known-unsent result, including those two defects, is timed Held with the per-item retry count. A deterministic invalid Follow can therefore repeat at the existing600-second cap; the Lead records that existing behavior separately, without adding a new terminal here.
