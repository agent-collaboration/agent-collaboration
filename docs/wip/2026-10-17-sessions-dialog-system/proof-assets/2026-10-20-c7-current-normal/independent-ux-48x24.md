## Confirmed P3 readability debt

Coordinates are approximate original-image pixels, origin top-left. These findings are nonblocking usability debt, not new architecture or feature requirements.

### P3-1 — Narrow browse separates facts from their natural label/value units

In browse and its repeated lower-credit/pace/top-return scenes, Status shows the reset symbol/count on one line and `resets` on the next. The Credits label, `62.5k` and `fresh` occupy three lines. Quota splits each percentage/remaining/reset duration into multiple lines. A reader can reconstruct the meaning, but must repeatedly move vertically and retain associations; this increases row height until the second account begins near the bottom.

- Annotation regions: Status approximately x225–321, y163–259; Quota x330–451, y164–322 in `browse`.
- This is not a broken-word or lost-data claim: fitting account words, `62.5k`, `7d` and `5h` remain intact, and all content stays in its semantic group. It does not contradict R10 merely because a record wraps.
- Smallest improvement to consider: keep the existing count/label and value/unit groupings together where physical space permits. Retain the three adopted groups and full identities; no side-by-side window redesign is proposed.
- Closure for this debt would be a current original making those associations easier to scan, followed by existing geometry/preservation proof owned by Main/V. No additional implementation is authorized by this report.

### P3-2 — Empty pace graphic adds noise despite explicit no-data meaning

In the same browse originals, the hollow-cell pace graphic wraps across lines before the words `burn unavailable`. A new reader sees a small broken-looking bar graphic before learning that it contains no usable measurement. The explicit wording prevents a false quantitative claim, but the graphic consumes scarce vertical space without helping the immediate decision.

- Annotation region: approximately x330–451, y345–402 in `browse`; equivalent area y385–442 for the selected Claude account in `next-account`.
- This is presentation debt, not missing forecast data or a request to invent a forecast. A simpler unavailable-state presentation could improve clarity within the existing ownership and information contract, if Root admits that work.
- Existing words remain understandable; this is not a proof gate for new functionality.
