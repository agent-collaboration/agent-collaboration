Requirements

In one sentence: agents the Router starts should just get on with their work; anything they aren't allowed to do should reach someone who can decide (a delegate's goes to whoever started it, anything else goes where the Router's setting says, by default to you through central); and every agent and script should reach the Router through three clean sets of tools that the CLI mirrors exactly.

Who this is for

You talk to one agent, the Lead. You or Sun start the Lead. The Lead starts delegate agents (an Implementer, a Verifier) through the Router; a delegate can't start sessions of its own, only its own subagents. Scripts, other agents and the CLI all reach the Router too.

What goes wrong today

A Claude delegate the Router starts begins in "ask me first" mode, so it stops for ordinary edits. It can't write to a temp folder without asking.

Your example: a Codex Verifier's own safety check refused to send a packet, twice. Nobody was told. The only fix was you opening that delegate's session and approving by hand.

About 165 tools in one list, mixing board content, messages and running agents. Session tools behave differently per agent. The CLI sometimes takes a different path than the tools.

What should happen instead

A delegate just works

Something it may not do reaches the Lead, then you

The delegate's approval shows at its parent (the Lead) and at any client watching it, all at once; the first answer wins. If the Lead can't decide, it forwards to central and you answer from the CLI (later your UI). Nothing is ever refused without someone deciding.

Three tool sets, and the CLI matches them

The three groups

You named these on 2026-11-13. Each need below belongs to one.

Start, steer, read and stop agents; delegate to delegates; route their approvals. Needs U1–U8.

Agents reaching agents: mail, wakes, schedules. Central checks who may do what. Need U9.

What a workspace holds: areas, projects, threads, posts, roles, deliverables, issues. Need U10.
