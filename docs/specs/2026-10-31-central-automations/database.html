Database cleanup (PR 1)

Every Router database runs in WAL mode, deletes old history on a clear schedule, and gives freed space back. This is the first PR in the stack, nearly done, and merges together with the automations PR.

What's kept, and for how long

AreaKept
Proxy (state.sqlite): response affinity30 days (OpenAI keeps responses at least that long)
Proxy: everything else1 week
Automation history (runs, deliveries, receipts, expired wakes, old instruction revisions)2 months; your schedules and instructions are never touched
Messages and interactions (finished ones)2 months; pending, held or in-flight messages are never pruned
Provider operations2 months; the session list is left alone
Session lifecycle and old addresses2 months; the 64 MB rebuild limit is removed
Fleet outbox2 months after finished
Board read cachedeleted once nothing is reading it (it was 925 MB on your Mac)
Project board fileskept forever

Getting space back

  • Plain SQLite (state, session registry, provider operations, automation): incremental vacuum, after a one-time conversion when the Router next starts. That conversion skips itself, with a warning, if free disk is short.
  • Turso (interaction and the board files): retention only for now. The pinned driver can't vacuum yet, and that's being researched.
  • Every database runs in WAL mode, including the two that were in DELETE mode.

Old board-snapshot install attempts can't be pruned until our agent-fleet-sync library adds a "finished at" time. That's a follow-up library change. The full list lives in docs/architecture/data-retention.md.
