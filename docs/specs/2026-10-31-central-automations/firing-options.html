Firing options and cost

As a planning model on the Workers Paid plan: about $7–8 per million firings when sent one at a time, about $5 with send-time batching (the route you chose). SQLite rows written are most of the cost; the alarm is noise. A busy setup, with 50 agents on 10-minute wakes around the clock, comes to about $1–2 a month. You have one organization today, and the multi-org figures are context for the separate service project.

What actually moves the cost

  1. Rows written per firing, about 80% of the cost. Record each firing's state once per transition, and don't journal the same fact on every hop.
  2. Batching at send time (your decision): firings for the same machine at the same grid point travel as one batch, sent right away.
  3. The 5-minute grid (your decision): firings due together share one alarm and one batch per machine.
  4. Batching reports. A machine's run reports go back once a minute instead of one per state change.
  5. Not the alarm count. One alarm is 1 request and 1 row, shared by everything due at that moment.
