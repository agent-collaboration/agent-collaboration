Central automations

Schedules and wakes become one central list for all your machines. Central keeps the clock on a 5-minute grid and sends each machine its due work in one batch. Each machine runs that work exactly as today and reports back. The same path also lets you create a session on another machine. Notifications reach each agent at most once every 10 minutes, and the databases clean up after themselves.

Design reviewed: ready (the Advisor's final verdict, with every finding closed). Remote sessions were added after that, and their review is under way. Your answers are in and building has started: see Your decisions.

Your decisions so far

  • Automations are central and never tied to a project.
  • Central fires at the due time, with send-time batching ("A"). Cancel happens in central only.
  • 5-minute grid; wakes every 10 minutes at most; notifications at most once per 10 minutes per agent.
  • Machines send to central in batches: every 500 ms or 100 items.
  • The limits live in the schema and are validated everywhere.
  • You can create sessions on other machines.
  • There's one organization today. Both PRs merge together.

Plan approved, with every milestone verified; 5-minute precision with no rounding; approvals and questions immediate; the 10-minute minimum for every wake; a named interim approver for remote sessions. See Your decisions.
