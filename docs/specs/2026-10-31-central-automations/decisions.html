Approvals, in three steps

Approval state lives only in interaction.sqlite; the JSON files go. Routes, meaning who created a session and who answers its prompts, eventually move to central so prompts can reach an approver on any machine.

StepWhereWhat
1Database PRapproval-routes.json is imported once into a table in interaction.sqlite, then deleted. The old approval-history.json code and file are removed.
2Automations PRNamed interim step: a remote create with no approver gets the target machine's Chief of Staff session, which you name once per machine. A machine with none named refuses before creating anything.
3PR 3, next after this stack, with its own designCentral owns the routes, prompts are sent through central to the approver's machine, and the requester becomes the default approver. Step 2's interim ends here. The Lead working on quota may take this design once quota is done.

Decided earlier

  • Automations are central definitions of message schedules, one list per organization; one organization for now.
  • Central fires on its own clock and batches per machine at send time (option A).
  • Cancel happens in central only; nothing chases a machine.
  • Machine-to-central reports go out every 500 ms or 100 items.
  • A 5-minute grid; no per-organization offset.
  • The limits live in the schema and in validation, so agents see them.
  • Cost measurement on staging waits until later.
  • Retention, vacuum and WAL on every database, with your retention table (PR 1).
