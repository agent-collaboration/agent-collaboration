Moving to central

The first time a machine runs the new release, it hands each of its schedules and live wakes to central, once. Each keeps its ID, timing, next due time, target, instruction and creator. During the move, exactly one side owns each automation's clock, so nothing fires twice and nothing is skipped.

The handover, per automation

  • A due time that falls during the move fires once: the local side stopped at "pending", and central catches up from the watermark under the fold rules.
  • A crash mid-move resumes at the same step. Central answers a repeat with alreadyImported, so no duplicate appears.
  • Ended wakes aren't moved; they stay as history.
  • A machine still on the old release never imports, so central holds nothing of its schedules and never fires them. It keeps working locally until it upgrades.
