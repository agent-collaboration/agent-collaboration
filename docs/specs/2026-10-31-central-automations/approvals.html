How approvals work

Approvals stay on the machine where the session runs. Nothing is sent to central or to another machine. A session's permission prompt is recorded in that machine's interaction.sqlite, delivered to the approver session on the same machine, and answered there. The database PR also moves the last approval data out of two JSON files into interaction.sqlite. Routing approvals across machines is a separate design for later.

One approval today, step by step

The route check refuses an approver or creator on another machine (native_approval.rs:18–22). That refusal is why approvals can't cross machines yet.
