  • Coordinate: Session/MCP Lead routing design: codex-router.feat-acp-cli-actions/docs/specs/2026-11-13-cli-mcp-sessions/specification.html#approvals. Their fan-out sits on this broker. The broker must settle each request exactly once (first decision wins) and answer later decisions with the existing not-pending refusal.
